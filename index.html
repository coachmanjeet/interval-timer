Interval Timer

Set it, press start, train. No account, no download.

First time? Start with Circuits, it names each move for you.

A round is one work period plus its rest. Get-ready counts down before work starts.

First time? Try 1 round. Work and rest apply to every station. 60 seconds rest between rounds. Tap a routine to start it. The timer names each station and shows what comes next.

Music and sound

Music starts at Go, not during get-ready. Rest periods switch to your rest pick automatically. These picks apply to both Timer and Circuits.

On iPhone, use the side buttons for music volume. This slider has no effect there.

Optional sound check. Tap Test sound: you should hear a tick, then the voice say "Go". START works without it. If you hear nothing, check "Timer sounds and voice on" is ticked, then the silent switch and volume.

Tip: on iPhone, Share > Add to Home Screen puts this on your home screen like an app.
